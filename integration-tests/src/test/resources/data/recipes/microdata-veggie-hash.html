Vegetarian Quick Dinner 2 PT10M PT15M PT25M
210 calories 9 grams fat 28 grams carbohydrates 6 grams protein
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat.
  2. Add the onion, pepper and garlic and fry until soft.
  3. Season well and serve hot.
